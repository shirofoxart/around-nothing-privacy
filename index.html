Privacy Policy

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the paid Android version of Around Nothing (package art.shirofox.aroundnothing), developed by shirofox.art. Last updated: November 3, 2026.

Data handled by the game

Around Nothing works offline and does not require an in-game account. The game does not send personal information, gameplay activity, device identifiers, or location to the developer. This version does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or developer-operated online services.

Local progress and settings

The game stores progress and preferences in its private storage on your device. These include particle balance, installed objects, upgrades, research, challenge and tutorial progress, orders, language, audio, and display settings. Local backup files help recover progress if a save is interrupted. These files are used to run the game and restore your session; they are not uploaded to the developer. The app disables Android Auto Backup and does not provide its own cloud save service.

Retention and deletion

Local progress is retained until you reset it or remove the app data. Use Settings → Erase all progress to reset gameplay progress; this keeps your preferences. To remove both progress and preferences, clear the app storage through Android Settings. Uninstalling normally removes local app data; any device-level transfer or backup behavior is controlled by your device and its settings.

Google Play

Purchases, installation, and any platform-level licensing checks are handled by Google Play. The game does not request or store your payment card details. Google may process purchase, device, and diagnostic information under its own privacy policy. Any sales or technical reports made available to the developer through Google Play are separate from data transmitted by the game itself.

Contact and support

If you email shirofox.art@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request and resolve support issues, and is retained only for as long as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the same address. Email is processed by the email service provider.

Children’s privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact shirofox.art@gmail.com so it can be reviewed and deleted as appropriate.

External services and this webpage

Links you choose to open outside the game are governed by the destination service’s privacy policy. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, when you visit it. This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies added by the developer.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact shirofox.art at shirofox.art@gmail.com.
